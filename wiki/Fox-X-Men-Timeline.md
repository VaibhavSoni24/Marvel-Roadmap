# 🧬 Fox X-Men Timeline Guide

The 20th Century Fox X-Men universe spans 18 films from 2000 to 2024. Because of time travel and continuity resets, many fans find the timeline daunting. Here is the canonical breakdown of how the franchise unfolds.

---

## 🧭 The Two Core Timelines

### Timeline A: The Original Tragic Timeline
1. **X-Men: First Class (1962)**: Charles Xavier and Erik Lehnsherr form the first mutant team.
2. **X-Men Origins: Wolverine (1979–1981)**: Logan receives his adamantium skeleton.
3. **X-Men (2000)**: Rogue meets Logan; Magneto attempts to mutate world leaders at Ellis Island.
4. **X2: X-Men United (2003)**: William Stryker attacks the mansion; Jean Grey sacrifices herself at Alkali Lake.
5. **X-Men: The Last Stand (2006)**: The Mutant Cure is discovered; Jean returns as the Dark Phoenix and is killed by Logan.
6. **The Wolverine (2013)**: Logan travels to Japan; post-credits scene reveals Charles and Erik recruiting Logan for an impending apocalyptic war.
7. **X-Men: Days of Future Past — Dystopian Future (2023)**: Sentinels have wiped out most of mutantkind. Kitty Pryde sends Wolverine's consciousness back to 1973.

---

### The Catalyst: *X-Men: Days of Future Past (1973)*
By preventing Mystique from assassinating Bolivar Trask in 1973, Wolverine, Charles, and Erik **erase the sentinel-dominated future**, branching reality into a brand-new timeline.

---

### Timeline B: The Revised Timeline
1. **X-Men: Days of Future Past — Past Events (1973)**: The Sentinel program is canceled; history is changed.
2. **X-Men: Apocalypse (1983)**: En Sabah Nur awakens; Jean Grey unleashes the Phoenix Force early.
3. **X-Men: Dark Phoenix (1992)**: Cosmic flare unleashes the alien Phoenix power.
4. **Deadpool (2016)**: Wade Wilson becomes Deadpool in a self-aware, post-reset world.
5. **Deadpool 2 (2018)**: Cable travels back in time; Wade repairs Cable's time-travel device.
6. **The New Mutants (2020)**: Demon Bear terrorizes young mutants in the Milbury facility.
7. **Logan (2029)**: Mutants have stopped being born due to genetic tampering; Logan sacrifices his life to save Laura (X-23).

---

## 🌌 Integration with the MCU Multiverse

### *Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)*
The Time Variance Authority (TVA) reveals that **Logan was the "Anchor Being" of Earth-10005 (the Fox Universe)**. His death in *Logan* started the slow decay of his universe.

Wade Wilson travels across the multiverse to find a replacement variant of Wolverine, ultimately saving his universe and bridging Earth-10005 into the wider MCU Multiverse landscape leading into *Avengers: Secret Wars* and *Avengers: Doomsday*.
