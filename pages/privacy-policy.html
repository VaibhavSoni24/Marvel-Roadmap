PRIVACY POLICY

Committed to Complete Privacy, Zero Tracking Cookies & Local Data Ownership

At Marvel Multiverse Roadmap (https://marvel-multiverse-roadmap.vercel.app/), we believe fan tools should respect user privacy. This policy explains what information is processed, where your data lives, and our strict commitment to zero third-party commercial advertising tracking.

Last updated: November 2026. This application was built by Vaibhav Soni as an open-source, non-commercial community project for Marvel fans worldwide.

1. Client-Side Local Storage (Your Data Stays on Your Device)

All personal progress tracking features in this application operate entirely inside your web browser using HTML5 localStorage:

  • Watched Titles: Saved under key marvel_roadmap_watched_v1 as an array of numeric IDs.
  • Skipped Titles: Saved under key marvel_roadmap_skipped_v1 as an array of numeric IDs.
  • Personal 1-10 Star Ratings: Saved under key marvel_roadmap_ratings_v1 as an object of ID-to-score mappings.

We do not store your watch history on remote databases. Your ratings and progress never leave your computer or phone. If you clear your browser cache or reset progress using the "Reset All Progress" button, this local data is permanently erased.

2. Real-Time Telemetry & Visitor Presence

Our live visitor counter at the top right communicates with a lightweight Vercel Serverless Function (/api/presence):

  • It generates an ephemeral, in-memory session identifier using your IP address prefix and user-agent string.
  • No session identifiers or IP addresses are written to permanent storage, databases, or log files.
  • Sessions automatically expire and are purged from serverless memory after 45 seconds of inactivity.
  • The endpoint returns only an anonymous aggregated integer representing active live sessions.

3. Third-Party Services & Embedded Media

To provide high-quality media, trailers, and fonts, the application interfaces with select third-party services:

  • YouTube (Privacy-Enhanced Mode): Video trailers are embedded via https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/. According to Google, this domain does not set cookies on your computer until you actively interact with the video player.
  • Google Fonts: Typography is delivered via Google Fonts CDN (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) to ensure consistent, responsive typography.
  • Vercel Edge Network: Our website is hosted globally on Vercel. Standard web server access logs (HTTP status, requested path, browser agent) may be processed in accordance with Vercel's global infrastructure privacy standards.
  • GitHub: Links to contributor profiles, code commits, and avatars are resolved via GitHub's public image CDN (https://github.com/<username>.png).

4. Voluntary Support Contributions

If you choose to support the project via UPI QR code or direct bank transfer on the Support page, transactions occur directly between your banking/UPI application and our receiving account. We do not operate a payment gateway, and we never collect, see, or store your debit/credit card numbers or banking passwords.
